## 1. Motivation: Separating Genuine Ontology Utility From Proxy Illusions

If unconstrained schema injection often hurts general Text2SQL ([Auto-Ontology Tier 3 regression](/experiments/2026-11-15-auto-ontology-postgres-vs-neo4j/)) and hurts generic construction (`LoG2026` Finding 1.4), **where do explicit ontologies genuinely earn their keep?** Auditing three independent local workspaces (`wt-h1-sdk`, `openup/seocho-onto-eng`, and `sol`) identifies two high-leverage regimes alongside two classic illusions:

1. **Temporal Prior Resistance (`sec-temporal`, `n=102`)**: Overcoming pretraining cutoff hallucinations on fresh FY2025 SEC 10-K numbers.
2. **Attribute Disambiguation (`sol` Exp 1 & 3, `n=200` each)**: Compressing noisy marketplace specs cleanly without hallucinated MOQ or benchmark figures—while checking if multi-agent decomposition helps (`sol` Exp 2).
3. **The Measurement Mirage (`opik-answerpath` & `gbench-keepraw`)**: Detecting when "graph accuracy" actually came from answer-text fallbacks rather than graph queries returning records.

## 2. Hypothesis, Falsification & Experimental Mechanism

We hypothesized that bounded domain schemas excel at post-cutoff temporal facts and dense attribute disambiguation once chunk fallbacks prevent empty-graph failures (`Mixed`: Exp 1 cuts prompts `−81.4%` past the `70%` target, whereas Exp 3 vs compact bigram RAG trims `−64.6%` (`1,244 → 440` tokens), just under `70%`, and `opik` reveals `0.30` true DB hit rate behind a `0.80` proxy).

## 5. Mechanism Breakdown: Why Bounded Attribute Maps & Chunks Coexist

1. **Concrete FY2025 prior failure (`sec-temporal` & Opik `65`-node subgraph)**: Without grounded context, `MiniMax-M2.5` explicitly refused or substituted FY2024 figures on Apple (`"FY2025 ... has not yet been completed ... reported $391.04 billion"` vs true `$416,161 million`) and Nvidia (`$96.7B` vs true `$215,938M`). Notably, inside the grounded log itself—mirroring our `65`-node Opik subgraph where only `3/10` (`0.30`) Cypher queries matched populated edges—structured Cypher returned empty results on several FY2025 revenue queries, meaning **`chunk_fallback=1` (vector chunks attached via Document nodes) rescued the `34/34` score** just as `keepraw` did on Novel (`0.191 → 0.495`). Meanwhile in the `n=16` answerability gate, strict abstention yielded `0/8` silent wrong answers on uncovered queries whereas closed-book vector retrieval leaked `3/8` hallucinations.
2. **Distinguishing genuine ontology cleanup from baseline disparity (`sol`)**: In `sol` Exp 1 (`−81.4%` tokens vs raw HTML/text listings), raw product pages mixed distractor benchmark noise with factory quotes, causing `35%` hallucination on `benchmark` queries (`65%` acc → `100%` under normalized JSON-LD, verified across `30` full trace audits in `audit_exp1_verify.jsonl`); against compact 500-char bigram RAG (Exp 3), JSON-LD eliminated `11%` hallucination cleanly (`100%` vs `88%`) and cut tokens `−64.6%` (`1,244 → 440`, slightly short of the `≥ 70%` Exp 1 benchmark bar). Meanwhile, Exp 2 suffered an artifact hygiene bug we surfaced during audit: a partial `RUN_ONLY=C` overwrite dropped product `P09` in `exp2_results.json` (`n=9` vs `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`'s Run 1 `n=10` `90%` / `4.23×` tokens), though both runs confirm multi-agent decomposition never beat unified `A`'s `90%`.

## 6. Scope Limits & Next Experiment

- **Toy vs production scale (`sol` & `opik`)**: SOL used synthetic marketplace fixtures averaging `2.3K` prompt tokens rather than real `48K+`-character 1688 DOM captures, and Exp 3 compared only against simple 500-char bigram RAG rather than dense hybrid retrieval + reranking.
- **Unaligned Answer-Path synonym trade-off**: In `opik-answerpath/grounding_embedding_isolated.txt`, synonym grounding tied baseline `4/5 vs 4/5`, fixing `location` while breaking `leadership`. Keeping raw chunks linked directly to canonical entity keys (`keepraw` + strict answerability refusal `0/8` silent wrong) remains the safest production pattern.
