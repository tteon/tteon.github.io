## 1. Motivation: The KV-Cache vs End-to-End Agent Reality Gap

Every LLM serving deck shows `10×–15×` TTFT wins from KV prefix caching. Yet when we built multi-turn GraphRAG agents across local GPUs (`H200`, `B200`, `RTX 3070`), production trace replays (`KCC 43K`), and hosted APIs (`MARA`), three hard bottlenecks repeatedly blunted those headline speedups: **LRU working-set eviction cliffs**, **decode/validator wall dominance (`82–93%`)**, and **cross-replica prefix fragmentation**.

## 2. Hypothesis, Falsification & Experimental Mechanism

We tested whether prefix caching and Graph-KV co-scheduling materially lower tail latency under multi-turn GraphRAG load (`Mixed`: massive prefill gains vanish at LRU cliffs and fail Holm gates under validator/decode walls).

  - `qwen_traceA_blksz_16`: `43,058` requests (`100.38M` input / `18.54M` output tokens; `53.58%` singleton blocks; `2.00 MiB`/block), replayed from `0.031 → 16.0 GiB` LRU. ## 5. Mechanism Breakdown: Why Caching Wins Pre-Fill Yet Fails to Rescue Tail E2E

1. **Non-linear LRU thrashing at saturation**: Prefix caching assumes hot blocks stay ahead of eviction. On `H200`, adding just `0.25%` headroom strain (`1,631,304` vs `1,635,338` KV capacity) turned `54.5K` `14.8×` speedups into near-zero (`0.2%`) reuse (`1.83 s` TTFT). On `B200` vLLM (`32K` KV), injecting a single `55,801`-token request evicted `100%` of the resident `2,000`-token prefix (`2,000 → 0`).
2. **Decode + validator serialization overwhelms graph DB latency**: In `real-stage-summary.json`, graph execution takes under `0.9` ms (`0.61–0.87` ms), whereas intent validation + dispatch takes `816–3,885` ms and decoding takes `78–1,122` ms. Even cleanly ordering queue entries (`B1/B2`) cannot clear the pre-registered Holm threshold (`p=0.625`) when external validator waits and serial autoregressive decode absorb `>99%` of request lifetime—and cutting evidence token budgets from `8,192` to `4,096` to speed decode immediately drops `answer_f1` from `0.717` to `0.574` and spikes partial truncations `7.5% → 42.5%`.
3. **Closed vs local API observability**: Hosted MARA probes returned empty `prompt_tokens_details={}` (`cache_usage={}`) from `2K` to `16K`, proving simulated reuse rates (`86.35%` hit in `LLMServingSim`) do not automatically translate across black-box API load balancers unless session affinity is explicit.

## 6. Key Findings & Transferable Insights

*Distilled via multi-agent deliberation (`Codex` + `Claude` from KV traces, H200 sweeps, and `e0_findings.md`):*

- **Prefill TTFT gains (`14.76×`) shrink by an order of magnitude at end-to-end wall level (`~1.19×`)**: While warm prefix caching slashes `54.5K` TTFT (`2.31 → 0.16 s`), full agent sessions saw end-to-end wall latency shift `10.30 → 8.62 s` (`~1.19×`) because autoregressive decode eats `82.2–92.5%` of request duration. **Rule:** Do not cut evidence budgets (`8,192 → 4,096`) to speed decode—F1 dropped `0.717 → 0.574` and partial truncations surged `7.5% → 42.5%`.
- **Size KV headroom against eviction cliffs, not infinite reuse assumptions**: `16 GiB` finite LRU hits collapsed `59.40% → 16.15%`, H200 hits crashed to `0.2%` at `99.75%` capacity, and one `55.8K` noisy turn evicted `100%` of a resident `2K` prefix. Start with `15–20%` capacity slack as an empirical policy and validate under mixed-length eviction stress.
- **Profile stage shares before building complex schedulers (`B0/B1/B2` `p=0.625`) & keep `E0` scoped honestly**: Graph lookup `<0.9 ms` cannot rescue `816–3,885 ms` validator+dispatch stalls, and `e0_findings.md` keeps status `PARTIAL` (280 transfer trials without calibrated DMA comparison endpoints—so shorter joint host critical paths are *not* claimed as proven hidden DMA).

## 7. Scope Limits & Blocked Hardware Work (`yw-pow.3`)

- **Blocked KV offload & prefetch API paths (`yw-pow.3`)**: `graphkv-cosched` policies `B3/B4/B5` remain `RESULT_PENDING` because vLLM lacks an external per-prefix GPU-prefetch hook; the `400`-case real QA matrix failed on Python `socket EPERM`; H200 `600 GB` LMCache offload hung >10 min; and RTX 3070 `layerwise=True` crashed on request #1.
- **E0 & Simulation boundaries**: `E0` trial blocks were not independent engine restarts, NUMA placement order was fixed (local then remote), observer overhead was unmeasured, and large-graph DRAM saturation remains untested (`e0_findings.md`). `LLMServingSim` substituted `Qwen3-30B-A3B` hardware LUTs for `gpt-oss` and `MiniMax` workloads due to unprofiled alternating/M2 layers.
