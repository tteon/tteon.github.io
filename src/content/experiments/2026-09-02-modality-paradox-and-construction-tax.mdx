## 1. Motivation: The Compression Illusion in GraphRAG

Knowledge graph pipelines are often justified on compression: why feed `370` tokens of messy 10-K prose when 7 structured triples (`24` tokens) capture the "essential facts"? To test whether structural compression preserves real answerability—and whether giving extractors an ontology schema improves graph consistency—we synthesize four datasets spanning `gnnllm_lab` (`n=2,542`), `icml2026` (`n=1,081`), `LoG2026-ready`, and `seocho-design/ama`.

## 2. Hypothesis, Falsification & Experimental Mechanism

We tested whether dense graph compression preserves answer sufficiency (`Refuted` on raw RDF/LPG and unconstrained construction taxonomies; verbalization partially rescues recall `3.4×`).

## 5. Mechanism Breakdown: Why Verbalization Succeeds Where Hop Expansion Fails

1. **Vocabulary mismatch, not missing node count, kills raw RDF**: Across `2,138 / 2,542` (`84.11%`) FinDER questions where RDF actually contained *more* or equal extracted elements than LPG, LPG still outperformed RDF on answer alignment `83.16%` to `5.66%`. Adding neighboring hops (`rdf_hop`: `7.39 → 21.23` units, `24 → 67` tokens) diluted precision (`0.092 → 0.061`) while barely nudging recall (`0.018 → 0.024`). Verbalizing the original `7.39` triples (`rdf_verb`, `48.3` tokens) restored grammatical relations and tokens, jumping IDF recall `3.4×` to match LPG (`0.0624` vs `0.0637`) with the highest precision (`0.2980`) of any mode.
2. **Why `text` beats all compressed graphs on FinDER**: Qualitative inspection (`compression_llm_judge.json`) reveals that financial QA routinely depends on exact numeric tables (`$1,557,813K` vs `$1,160,403K` EBIT across 2021–2023) and explicit causal phrasing (`"due to higher sales"`) that triple extractors drop across `icml`'s `n=1,081` audit (averaging `211.6` text tokens vs `9.0` graph facts = `23.5×` corpus ratio, `61.1×` mean per-question compression ratio).
3. **Why rigid construction ontologies increase empty graphs**: In `LoG2026` Finding 1.4 (`16` cases) and `sota_construction_v2`, injecting 70+ taxonomy classes gives smaller or safety-tuned models (`MiniMax-M2.7` parse dropping `85% → 50%` on Medical) too many ways to split facts or exceed schema constraints, turning clean spans into empty graphs (`15% → 29.2%`). Similarly, in `LoG2026` `rebuttal/path_query_control_v2` (`n=120`), corrupting graph edges collapsed complex path exactness `1.000 → 0.000`, proving structure matters downstream *only* when extraction survives intact and stays grounded.

## 6. Scope Limits & Next Experiment

- **Missing LLM-as-Judge on `rdf_verb`**: The `2026-03-01` `rdf_hop`/`rdf_verb` comparison scored lexical IDF overlap rather than full LLM-as-Judge sufficiency; running `gpt-oss-120b` judge scoring on `rdf_verb` is queued in Beads `yw-pow.7`.
- **Paused 420-case full retrieval**: `LoG2026-ready/finder_graphrag_sota_v1/full` (`420` cases) was paused after worker overlap wrote `5,849` graph calls (`yw-pow.6`), leaving bounded `rebuttal100` (`n=100`, `passage_tfidf R@5 0.530` vs `hipporag 0.390` vs `gr_pcst 0.320`) as the cleanest comparable retrieval baseline.
