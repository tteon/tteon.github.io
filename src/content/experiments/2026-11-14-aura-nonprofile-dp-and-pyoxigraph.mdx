## 1. Motivation & System Bottleneck

Our earlier [Aura runtime study](/experiments/2026-11-14-neo4j-aura-runtime-routing/) used `PROFILE` queries and reported a `4.11×` speedup on heavy global 3-hop analytics (`W4`). Yet three practical questions remained unanswered in follow-up scripts left locally across `~/lab/aura-runtime/study/` and `~/lab/seocho-rust/`:

1. How much of Slotted's `2.3 s` latency came from `PROFILE` row-counting hooks rather than actual query execution?
2. Can schema constraints and query rewrites beat runtime hints altogether—and are they semantically identical?
3. When a full Graph-CoT or tool-calling agent queries an external graph DB (`Neo4j`) versus an embedded RDF store (`PyOxigraph`), does database execution even matter compared to LLM turn time?

## 3. Environment & Reproducibility Protocol

- **Aura Enterprise Study (`aura-runtime/study/`)**: AuraDB Pro managed instance (Neo4j `5.27-aura` Enterprise, `4` workers, `544 MiB` page cache), `5,000` nodes / `40,000` edges + `5,000`-edge hub (`8` self-loops). Ran `timing_bench.py` (`10` randomized rounds × 6 workloads × 3 runtimes = `180` non-PROFILE runs), `optimize_lab.py` (`7` property/type/key constraints + `5` constraint-sensitive plans + W4 DP rewrite), and `monitor_lab.py` (`50 ms` `SHOW TRANSACTIONS` poll + lock termination).
- **Manufacturing FMEA (`seocho-rust/experiments/mfg_ontology_bench/`)**: `1,362` quads in PyOxigraph vs Neo4j across 5 FMEA scenarios (6 read queries + 2 writes), closed-loop concurrency `c=1..64` on `L1580`, plus `32`-question `gpt-oss-120b` tool-agent loadtest.
- **Graph-CoT Read-Path (`seocho-rust/experiments/graphcot_readpath/`)**: Healthcare KG (`n=270` questions, `2,046` tool calls incl. `1,529` DB calls). *(Note: Neo4j Enterprise scale extensions in `auto-ontology-bench` remain excluded per Neo4j AUP and cataloged openly under Beads `yw-pow.6`.)*

## 5. Mechanism Breakdown: Why Constraints & Semantics Trump Flags

- **Why `PROFILE` fooled our first benchmark**: Enabling per-operator row/hit telemetry adds instrumentation work disproportionately inside `Slotted`'s row-at-a-time volcano loop (`2,186` ms vs `1,226.5` ms server time, inflating apparent server speedup to `5.38×` over `406` ms Parallel and client-wall to `4.11×` vs true non-PROFILE `2.46×`). Conversely, `Parallel` worker threads fail to update transaction-level `pageHits` in `SHOW TRANSACTIONS`, printing `0` hits during active 3-hop execution—so production alerts on `pageHits` would falsely assume zero cache reads.
- **Why hop-by-hop pre-aggregation breaks exact equality (`1,791,878` vs `1,792,000`)**:

```cypher
MATCH (c:AuraRuntimeLabNode)-[:AURA_LAB_LINK]->(d:AuraRuntimeLabNode) WHERE d.score >= 30
WITH c, count(*) AS n, sum(d.score) AS s
MATCH (b:AuraRuntimeLabNode)-[:AURA_LAB_LINK]->(c) WITH b, sum(n) AS n, sum(s) AS s
MATCH (a:AuraRuntimeLabNode)-[:AURA_LAB_LINK]->(b) WITH a.segment AS segment, sum(n) AS path_count, sum(s) AS ss
RETURN segment, path_count, toFloat(ss)/path_count AS mean_score
```

By breaking a 3-hop `MATCH (a)-[]->(b)-[]->(c)-[]->(d)` pattern into three `WITH` clauses, each step forgets previously traversed relationship IDs. That reduces Slotted execution from `1,244` ms to `77` ms (`22.5×` fewer DB hits), **but turns Cypher's default trail semantics (edge uniqueness within one pattern) into walk semantics (edge revisits allowed across 8 self-loops)**. Running the original single pattern under `CYPHER 25 MATCH REPEATABLE ELEMENTS` reproduced `1,792,000` identically.
- **Why DB speed barely moves multi-turn Graph-CoT**: Across `n=270` healthcare questions (`2,046` tool calls / `2,182` LLM turns), single-turn DB lookups take `2.79` ms p50 (`p99 = 27.11` ms) versus `1,151.7` ms p50 LLM turns (`412×` slower), and total accumulated DB wait across episodes (`stall_share_wall`) is just **`0.47%`** (`[0.44%–0.50%]`; server-only share `2.13%`). Removing 100% of DB latency would trim under `0.5%` of end-to-end wall time unless concurrency pushes Neo4j above its ~8-core CPU saturation knee (`265.7` req/s on `L1580`).

## 6. Scope Limits & Next Experiment

- **Graph scale & Enterprise boundaries**: Aura lab (`5K` nodes) fits entirely inside the `544 MiB` page cache, whereas Neo4j Enterprise multi-hundred-thousand-node scale and tuning sweeps remain withheld per Neo4j AUP restrictions (`yw-pow.6`).
- **Write skew & unexecuted Graph-CoT arms**: Concurrent writes in `L1580` produced duplicate observations under `c >= 32` without unique merge locking, and the 8-graph local latency ladder (`sr-tlc.3`) plus Vast `gpt-oss-120b` co-location (`sr-tlc.4`) remain open in `seocho-rust/.beads`.
